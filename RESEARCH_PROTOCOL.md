# Manual School Research Protocol

When researching schools manually (outside the app), apply these rules IN ORDER and stop as soon as a disqualifying condition is met.

## Schools near Chalfont PA (always fully research — skip Rule 1)
- Villanova
- Bryn Mawr

## Rule 1 — Check online student eligibility FIRST
Search: "[school] student health insurance plan online distance students eligible"

If online/distance students are **explicitly excluded** → mark as SKIP, record reason, STOP. Do not research IVF.

If online students are eligible or unclear → continue to Rule 2.

## Rule 2 — Check IVF / fertility coverage
Search: "[school] student health insurance plan fertility IVF coverage"

If ANY of the following are explicitly **excluded**:
- IVF / in vitro fertilization
- IUI / artificial insemination
- Oocyte retrieval
- Ovulation stimulation / ovulation induction (blanket)
- Infertility treatment (blanket)

→ mark IVF = No, record quote, STOP.

## Only if both rules pass → full research
Collect: plan year, state, cycles, verbatim IVF language, min credits, credit language, all source URLs.
